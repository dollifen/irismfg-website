Manufacturing Excellence

IRIS Manufacturing Ltd. stands as a beacon of quality in the personal care industry. Located in the heart of Jiangxi Province's industrial park, our state-of-the-art facility spans 100,000 square meters across 8 specialized workshops.

With a $43 million investment and 2 professional assembly lines, we deliver an annual production capacity of 50,000 tons — serving global brands with OEM and ODM solutions that exceed FDA and EEC compliance standards.
